Dear IJCAI 2026 Proceedings Team,

We were referred to you by the demo track chairs regarding the editorial review of our paper

**#DM234 — LLARS: Enabling Domain Expert & Developer Collaboration for LLM Prompting, Generation and Evaluation** (Demo Track)

We have implemented all editorial requirements. For Figure 3, however, we are not entirely sure what exactly is being asked of us and would like to keep the figure as originally submitted. The editorial note states the general criteria ("text is not too small, graph's bars/lines are distinguishable enough when printed in grayscale") and refers to Figure 3, without specifying which of the two aspects is at issue.

More importantly, the purpose of Figure 3 is to show *what the provenance-analysis view in \textsc{Llars} looks like* — it is a screenshot of the feature, not a data plot. The reader is not expected to read off individual bar values or to compare buckets numerically; the figure exists so that readers can recognise the view when they try the demo. Whether the three bars in each mini-chart are individually distinguishable in grayscale is therefore not load-bearing for the message of the paper.

On top of that, the legend at the top of the figure lists the buckets ("Good", "Medium", "Bad") in the same fixed left-to-right order as the bars in every panel, so the bar-to-bucket assignment is still recoverable from position alone even when printed in grayscale.

For these reasons we would like to leave Figure 3 unchanged. If you require a modification nonetheless, please let us know and we will adjust accordingly.

Thank you very much for your time.
